Course topics

Fifteen labs in three parts: search, machine learning and language models.

LabAt a glanceWhat it is about
Part 1 · Search and reasoning
01Uninformed Search State spaces and blind search: BFS, DFS, uniform-cost and iterative deepening.
02Informed Search Heuristics guide the search: greedy best-first, A* and its variants.
03Constraint Satisfaction Variables, domains and constraints: backtracking, AC-3 and min-conflicts.
04Genetic Algorithms Evolution as search: selection, crossover, mutation and a population of candidates.
05Games Adversarial search: game trees, minimax and alpha-beta pruning.
Part 2 · Machine learning
06Introduction to ML Learning from data: regression vs classification, training vs test, bias and variance.
6.5Linear & Logistic Regression Fitting a line with gradient descent, then turning it into probabilities with the sigmoid.
07K-Nearest Neighbours Classify a point by a vote of its closest neighbours: distances, scaling and choosing K.
08Naive Bayes Classifier Bayes' theorem with an independence assumption: spam filtering and Laplace smoothing.
09Decision Trees Split the data with yes/no questions: entropy, information gain, ID3 and pruning.
10K-Means and Clustering Finding groups without labels: K-means, K-means++, silhouette and hierarchical clustering.
11Neural Networks From one neuron to multilayer networks: activations, gradient descent and back-propagation.
Part 3 · Language models
12Transformers and Generative Models How LLMs work: tokens and embeddings, self-attention, and generating text one token at a time.
13Post-training and Alignment From a pretrained model to an assistant: fine-tuning, reward models, RLHF and DPO.
14Evaluation, Metrics and Data Analysis Measuring what matters: precision and recall, text metrics, LLM judges, and reading plots critically.

How the labs work

  1. Class notes: every lab has a page with the definitions, worked examples and “Try it” widgets you can play with.
  2. Quizzes: a lab is followed by an auto-graded quiz with an explanation for every answer. Answers stay in your browser; nothing is collected.
  3. Menu: the left menu (☰ on phones) lists every lab; switch between Class notes and Quizzes at its top.
  4. Notebooks: the Jupyter notebooks for the labs are in the course repository.
